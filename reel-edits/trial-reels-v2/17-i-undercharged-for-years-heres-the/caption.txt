When only you can deliver the work, every sale is a promise about your own time. Charging more feels like promising more of yourself, so you hold back. The price ends up capped by your capacity.

Once delivery is designed so the business delivers it, you're selling a defined outcome with a process and a team behind it.

Try this: write what the client gets at the end, in their words. List every step to get there and mark the ones only you can do. If most are marked, start there before you touch the price. It's a design problem, and design is fixable.
