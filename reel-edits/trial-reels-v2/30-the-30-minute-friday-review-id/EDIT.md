## Caption
Ten minutes on numbers: cash in the bank, cash due in 30 days, delivery hours, conversations booked.

Ten minutes on what reached you this week that shouldn't have. Pick one and give it a new home: a rule, a recording, a role card or a person.

Ten minutes on next week. Deep work first, then client time, then admin. Protect at least one half day with nothing in it.
