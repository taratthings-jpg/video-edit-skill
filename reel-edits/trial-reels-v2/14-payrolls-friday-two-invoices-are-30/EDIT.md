## Caption
Cash flow trouble in a service business usually comes from timing, and from the founder being the only one who chases.

Three fixes cover most of it. Invoice on delivery day, every time, from a template. Put payment terms and a late fee in the contract, and enforce them. Hand chasing to someone else with a script, because a reminder from operations gets paid faster than an awkward one from you.

Then every Friday, list what's due in and out over the next four weeks. Ten minutes.
