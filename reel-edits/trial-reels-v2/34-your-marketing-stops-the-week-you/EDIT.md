## Caption
If your marketing needs your face, your voice and your evenings, it stops the first busy week. The pipeline dries up two months later.

The rhythm I trust: one hour a week of your thinking, as a voice memo answering what clients actually ask. Someone else turns that hour into posts, a newsletter and clips. One shared calendar shows what's going out and who owns it. Your job shrinks to the hour and a quick approval.
