## Caption
Each question takes a couple of minutes, plus a few more to get back into what you were doing. Call it five. Fifteen a day is 75 minutes, gone in fragments.

Try office hours. Two fixed windows a day, say 1pm and 4pm, when the team brings you anything. Outside them, questions go into one shared place, written down. Agree three situations that can interrupt you anytime.

Within a fortnight, half the questions get answered by the person asking, because writing them down makes them think.
