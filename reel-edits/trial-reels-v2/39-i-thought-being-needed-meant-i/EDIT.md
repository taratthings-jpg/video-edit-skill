## Caption
Being the person everyone came to felt like proof. That I was good, that the business needed me, that I mattered.

It took me years to see the cost. A business that needs you for everything can't grow past what you can carry.

An honest question: if your team could handle most of what reaches you this week, would you feel relieved or a little lost? If lost, that's worth noticing. The business and your identity have grown into each other, and part of redesigning the business is letting them separate.
