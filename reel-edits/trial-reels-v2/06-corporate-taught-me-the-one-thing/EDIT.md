## Caption
After my agency, I led communications and marketing at a sustainable materials company. The biggest lesson was about who decides what.

In a well-run company every recurring decision has an owner. In most founder-led businesses, everything goes to the founder because she's the only safe answer.

Build the map in an afternoon. List every decision that reached you in the last two weeks, and next to each, who should own it and the rule they'd follow. Discounts under a set amount. Scope changes under a set number of hours.

Comment SYSTEM and I'll send you The 7 Boring Systems. System 2 is the Decision Rules.
