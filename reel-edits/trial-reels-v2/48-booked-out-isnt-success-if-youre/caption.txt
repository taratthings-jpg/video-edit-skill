“Fully booked until March” sounds like the dream. If every booking needs you personally, it's a waiting list for your exhaustion.

Being fully booked is the best moment to redesign, because demand finally gives you options. Raise the price on a defined offer. Bring someone in to deliver part of it. Say no to work that doesn't fit.

Ask which parts of what you're booked for genuinely need you. Usually the strategy, the key conversation and the final review. The rest is process.
