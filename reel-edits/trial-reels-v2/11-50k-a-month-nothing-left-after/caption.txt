Founders tell me this quietly. The revenue looks great. The balance on the 28th says otherwise.

Usually it's three small leaks. Pricing set when the business was smaller. Delivery that takes more hours than the price assumed. Costs that crept up one tool at a time.

A 20-minute check: last month's revenue minus every cost, including what you should have paid yourself. That's your real margin. Then divide revenue by total delivery hours. If the margin is under 20%, the fix is rarely more sales.
