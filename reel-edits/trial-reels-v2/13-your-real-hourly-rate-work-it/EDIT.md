## Caption
Take what you paid yourself last month. Divide it by every hour you worked, evenings and weekend messages included.

$8,000 across 240 hours is about $33 an hour. For a founder running a $40K-a-month business, that number usually stings.

A low founder rate means one of three things: you're underpaying yourself, you're doing work someone else could do, or the business needs your hours to function. Usually it's the third, and that's a design question.
