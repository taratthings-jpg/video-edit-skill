When I ran my agency, I paid everyone before me. Contractors, tools, rent, tax. Me, if anything was left.

The rule I'd set past $30K a month: fix your founder pay as a monthly amount and move it on the same day every month, before anything discretionary. It's payroll, so treat it that way.

If the business can't cover it comfortably, that's information. The price is too low, delivery is too heavy, or costs have crept.
