## Caption
AI is brilliant at following a process and poor at guessing one. If proposals, onboarding or reporting live only in your head, a tool gives you a faster version of the confusion.

The order that works: write the process with the steps, the standard and one example of good. Test it with a person following the page. Then hand the repeatable parts to AI: drafting, summarising, formatting, first passes.

Start with a recap after client calls. Write the template, then let AI fill it from your notes.
