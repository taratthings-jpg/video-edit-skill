When founders feel stretched, the instinct is to hire. Often that adds a manager to your week, and the manager is you.

Count first. For one week, tally every decision you make for the business: pricing a custom request, approving a design, choosing a supplier, deciding which client goes first.

Then sort them. Which could a written rule decide? Which disappear with one standard offer? Which belong to a role that already exists? The hire you need after that is usually different from the one you planned.
