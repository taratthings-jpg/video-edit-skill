## Caption
Sport taught me discipline and how to push through. Both made me a good founder, for a while.

What I forgot is that athletes never train without recovery. Rest days are scheduled. The programme decides, not the mood on the day.

Your business can run the same way. Block deep work, batch admin, and keep one day where nothing reaches you. Plan the week once and let the plan carry you.

Comment SYSTEM for The 7 Boring Systems. System 7 is the Designed Week.
