## Caption
Some people will ask why you're not hiring faster, why you turned down a client who didn't fit, why you're not working weekends when things are going well.

In practice it looks like one offer defined clearly, a team that owns decisions, numbers checked every Friday and a week designed around deep work and real rest. None of it is flashy. All of it compounds.

I spent years building the other way, through my own agency and 80+ brands. I know which version I'd choose again.
