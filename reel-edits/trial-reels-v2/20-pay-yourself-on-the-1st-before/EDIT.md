If founder pay is whatever's left, you're the shock absorber for the business. Good months you overpay without a plan. Bad months you skip it and feel it at home.

Set a fixed monthly amount and move it on the same date as payroll. Put tax aside in a separate account at the same time. Build a two-month cash reserve before any new hire or big spend.

The pressure is useful. It shows quickly whether your prices, delivery and costs actually work.
