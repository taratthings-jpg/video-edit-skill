## Caption
Right question, asked in the wrong order. First ask what the hire will own: outcomes and decisions, beyond tasks. Someone who owns client onboarding end to end frees your week. Someone who “helps with admin” usually adds a manager to your job, and the manager is you.

Then cost it properly: salary, tools, onboarding, and your own hours training them for two months. That last line is the one founders miss.

Document the role before they start. A one-page role card, a recorded walkthrough, and what they can decide alone.
