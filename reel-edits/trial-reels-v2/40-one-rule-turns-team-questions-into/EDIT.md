## Caption
Nothing reaches me without three things: what happened, the options, your recommendation.

It moves the thinking to the person closest to the problem. Half the time, writing the options makes the answer obvious and the question never arrives. The other half, you get a decision that's 80% made.

Introduce it gently. For two weeks, when a question arrives without the three parts, reply: “What are the options, and what would you recommend?” Then wait.
