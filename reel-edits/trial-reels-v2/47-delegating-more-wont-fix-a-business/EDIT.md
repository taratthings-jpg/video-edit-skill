## Caption
You delegated. It came back. You delegated again, more carefully. It came back again.

Delegation breaks in three predictable places. No written standard, so the work comes back for checking. No decision rule, so the person asks before acting. No ownership, so they're doing tasks while you still hold the outcome.

Fix those first. Write what good looks like with one example. Write what they can decide without you. Hand over the whole outcome. Then let them get it slightly wrong a couple of times.
