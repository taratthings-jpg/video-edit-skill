## Caption
New clients come in, each slightly different. Delivery takes longer than the price assumed. A contractor, then a tool, then evenings again. Each new dollar costs more than the last.

Look at profit per client. Take your last ten: fee, delivery hours, direct costs. You'll usually find two or three making most of the profit and a few quietly losing money.

Then design around the profitable ones. What did they buy? How was it delivered? Make that the standard offer.
