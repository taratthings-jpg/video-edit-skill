Answers that live in old messages can't be found, so people ask you.

Start a shared document called How we decide. Every time you answer something that could come up again, add one line: the situation, the answer and the reason. Group by clients, money, delivery and tools.

Then one team habit: check the document before asking. If it isn't there, ask, and whoever gets the answer adds it. Within six weeks most repeat questions disappear.
