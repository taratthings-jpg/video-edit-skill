## Caption
I grew up as an athlete. Work harder, train longer, push through. It works in sport for a while, and in business for a while too.

Then it stops working, and most founders respond by working harder still.

My own agency and years of work on other people's brands taught me that freedom is the result of building differently. A business that grows without depending on you has to be designed that way.

One place to start: find the single thing that pulled you back in most often last month. A decision, a client, a task, a login. Design that one thing out.

Comment AUDIT and I'll send you the free Founder Dependency Audit. It shows you where to start.

New here? I'm Tara, founder of Aluna. Follow for freedom by design.
